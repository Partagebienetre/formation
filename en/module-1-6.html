The co-founders
Hilde & Örjan Sæle
Co-founders of Zinzino · Norway · 2005
Hilde Sæle
Co-founder & Global Top Leader
A former teacher, Hilde embodies Zinzino’s mission: bringing out the best in everyone. She built the company with heart, putting human relationships at the centre of everything. Convinced that change begins with a first courageous step, she now guides thousands of partners around the world. Her commitment: giving people the tools to change their lives.
Örjan Sæle
Co-founder & Architect of the network
A determined Norwegian entrepreneur, Örjan started the Zinzino adventure in 2005 with a clear vision: improving health through informed decisions, not guesswork. His philosophy — leave nothing to chance — has put Zinzino at the forefront of test-based personalised nutrition. He has built a worldwide network of more than 40,000 independent partners on five continents.
The story — from a tent to 20 million
2005 — The beginnings
Örjan and Hilde start their company in Norway as the exclusive sales agency for a Franco-Belgian coffee brand. Norway and Sweden open first, followed by the whole of Scandinavia.
2012 — The health turning point
Hilde and Örjan join BioActive Foods, a Norwegian R&D company specialising in Omega-3. BalanceOil is born, and with it the BalanceTest — undeniable third-party proof that the product meets real, documented needs.
2014 — Stock market listing
Zinzino is officially approved for listing on Nasdaq First North. The New York Stock Exchange bell rings — a pledge of full financial transparency. The accounts are public and can be checked by anyone.
2015 — European expansion
The range grows: coffee, nutritional supplements, skincare, well-being. Zinzino opens offices in Riga and Florida. European expansion accelerates.
2016 — 100,000 active customers
A symbolic milestone: 100,000 active customers worldwide. Partners on every continent grow their business with exclusive rights to promote test-based nutrition.
2021 — Global webshop & SEK 1.3 bn
Launch of the global webshop, new markets opened in Asia: 99 markets covered. Turnover of 1.3 billion Swedish kronor reached — a historic milestone.
2023 — 1 million blood tests
Historic milestone: 1 million BalanceTests completed. The independently managed dried blood spot database is now the largest in the world.
2024 — 100+ countries, 5 continents
Acquisitions of Xelliss (Luxembourg) and Cleanthi (Cyprus). Launch of Collagen Boozt. Zinzino is now present in more than 100 countries on five continents.
2025 — 20 years & looking ahead
Celebrating 20 years of leadership. Acquisition of Zurvita (USA). Gut health becomes a key focus. More than 1,400,000 BalanceTests completed and 40,000 partners worldwide. The goal: to become the global reference in personalised preventive health.

What this means for you
🎯 Why the company story is a sales tool
When a prospect asks you “is this serious?”, you have concrete answers: listed on the stock market, 20 years in business, independent laboratory, present in 40 countries, measurable results. You don’t represent an unknown start-up — you represent a solid company with a long-term vision and tangible proof of its seriousness.
My actions
  • I know what the word “Zinzino” means
  • I know the vision (20M customers by 2035) and the mission (Inspire Change in Life)
  • I can tell the company story in 2 minutes
  • I can explain why the stock market listing and the independent lab show the company is serious
