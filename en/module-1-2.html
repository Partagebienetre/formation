Why the Why?

“When the Why is strong, the How becomes easy.”

— Oslo Convention 2025

Prospecting techniques, invitation scripts — all of that can be learned. But without a deep reason to keep going when things get hard, the best techniques in the world won’t help. Your Why is the engine. Everything else is just fuel.

A leader’s testimony: “Without a mission… where are we going?” The path gets blurry and it’s easy to lose your way when you no longer know why you’re moving forward.
The 5 Whys technique

Ask yourself the question five times in a row. The 5th answer is almost always the real one.

📖 How it works
Start with: “Why am I doing this business?” — then take each answer and ask: “And why does that matter to me?” Repeat five times.
