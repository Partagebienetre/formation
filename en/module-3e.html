Why reading is a professional activity

“I spent more than 1,000 hours receiving direct instruction from Jim Rohn — 99% of it through his books and audio programmes. Wherever you are, you can benefit from a mentor’s teaching if they have put their ideas into a book.”

— Darren Hardy, The Compound Effect

Training doesn’t stop at what you teach your team. It starts with what you take in yourself. A leader who doesn’t read relies only on their own past experience. A leader who reads has the experience of thousands of people.

The 20-minute rule: Spend at least 20 minutes a day reading about personal or professional development. Over a year, that’s 12 to 15 books. In 5 years, you’ll have taken in more practical wisdom than most people gather in a lifetime.
The 5 fundamental principles of network marketing
1
You are in the prospecting business
Not in the product business. Your job is to create new qualified contacts all the time. Accept it and never forget it.
2
The system does the work — not you
Your role is to bring people in front of the system (presentation, Zoom, tool). The system convinces. You don’t have to explain everything yourself.
3
Leverage is the key
You multiply your impact by training people who do what you do. One tree doesn’t make a forest — a seed that replicates does. That’s the principle of the network.
4
Consistency beats talent
The compound effect of small regular actions always beats sporadic, intense efforts. Three calls a day, 5 days a week, 52 weeks a year = 780 conversations.
5
You become what you read and listen to
Your mental environment shapes your results. Choose your influences deliberately: books, podcasts, the people around you. That choice is the most important one in your business.
