The SMART method
SSpecificPrecise and clearly defined. Not “earn more” but “reach €2,000 in monthly commissions”.
MMeasurableA number, a date, a concrete indicator telling you whether the goal is reached.
AAchievableAmbitious but realistic. It should stretch you without discouraging you.
RRealisticAligned with your current resources, your available time and your life context.
TTime-boundA precise deadline. “By 31 December” is infinitely better than “soon”.
