Why plan?

“If you want to be free, you have to present a concept every day, every single day.”

— Oslo Convention 2025

Partnering adapts to your life — you set your working time and your goals. But that freedom has a price: without a schedule, good intentions disappear. The book, the friend who calls, the children, tiredness — everything conspires against your evening calls if you haven’t blocked that time in advance.

The golden rule: What goes into the schedule exists. What isn’t there will always be pushed aside by the urgency of the moment.

🚂 The locomotive metaphor

When it’s standing still, a simple block of wood is enough to stop a locomotive. But once it’s going at 90 km/h, it can smash through a 1.5 m reinforced concrete wall. Your routines are the steam that drives the train. The first weeks feel hard — that’s normal, the locomotive starts slowly. But once you have momentum, nothing can stop you.
