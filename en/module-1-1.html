The decisive moment

“That decisive moment doesn’t come when you feel confident or comfortable. It comes when you decide. When you decide to go all in, when you decide this is what you want to do.”

— A leader’s testimony, Oslo Convention 2025

Starting a business doesn’t begin with a technique or a script. It begins with a decision — clear, conscious, irrevocable. Not a decision made when everything is perfect, but a decision made now, despite doubts and uncertainty.

Remember: Total commitment doesn’t mean the absence of fear. It means fear is no longer in charge. You have decided, and that decision comes before any action.
The 3 levels of commitment
🏃 Level 1 — Hobby mode
You try, you fumble, you see if it works. Results are random, investment is minimal. This level rarely leads to lasting success.
💼 Level 2 — Serious mode
You work regularly, train your partners, track results. Income grows. But part of you still keeps an exit door open.
💎 Level 3 — All-in mode
This is where everything changes. You no longer ask “if” it will work, but “how” and “when”. Exponential results start here.
My commitment statement

Putting your commitment in writing is a powerful act. Fill in your personal statement below — it will be saved in your browser.

✍️ My Personal Statement

“Today I decide to commit fully to this business. I am no longer trying — I have decided. I commit to becoming the best version of myself and to building something great for my family and for the people I will support.”

  • I have filled in and saved my commitment statement
  • I have identified my level of commitment (1, 2 or 3)
  • I have shared my decision with my sponsor or someone I trust
