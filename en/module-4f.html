🚀 Module 4 · Chapter f

The
Events

Events are the glue of your network. They create social proof, strengthen the conviction of those who hesitate and give your prospects the chance to see for themselves.

Why events are essential

“You can present a product online. But you connect hearts when you are with people.”

— Oslo Convention 2025
🎤
Social proof
Seeing other people share their experience is 10 times more powerful than you explaining on your own.
🤝
Human connection
A network is built on relationships. Events create lasting bonds between partners.
📈
Duplication
Your partners see how you invite — they duplicate. The event trains them without anyone teaching.
💡
Conviction
A hesitant prospect who attends a well-run zoom often decides that same evening.
🏆
Recognition
Ranks announced, experiences shared — public recognition is a powerful driver.
📅
Rhythm & consistency
Regular events create a rhythm of activity. Without rhythm, there is no growth.

How to use the zooms for your prospects
📞
Invite before the zoom
Build up the event and the speakers before sending the link. “One of my leaders is giving a very complete presentation on Tuesday evening at 8:30 — they are in high demand.”
✅
Get a commitment
“If I send you the link, will you join on Tuesday at 8:30?” — a concrete verbal commitment is worth far more than an “I’ll see”.
🔄
Confirm the day before
A quick message the day before the zoom greatly increases attendance. “Reminder for tomorrow evening at 8:30 — see you then!”
🤫
Stay in the room
During the zoom, stay quiet. Your part is done. Let the speaker present. Take notes and prepare your follow-up.
💬
Follow up within 24h
“What did you think of the presentation?” — Following up within 24 hours of the zoom is crucial. That is where the decision is made.
🔁
Get your team into the habit
Invite your partners to these zooms as soon as they start. They see how it is done and duplicate naturally. It is training by example.

How to prepare your team for Barcelona

Decide and register yourself first

The 48 to 72 hours after a big event are the most powerful of the year. Everyone is inspired, convinced, enthusiastic. It is the ideal time to reconnect with all your lukewarm contacts and send your new invitations.
Following up after each event — zoom or in person
🔄 The 24-hour rule — The follow-up that makes the difference
During the event — Note the names of the prospects attending. Watch their reactions. Prepare your follow-up message.

Within 24h — Contact each prospect who attended: “What did you think of this evening?” Let them speak. Listen before talking.

If interested — Set the next meeting immediately. Don’t let the momentum drop. “When shall we meet to talk about it?”

If hesitant — Invite them to the next zoom. “The next presentation is on Tuesday — shall we watch it together?”

If absent — Send a gentle message: “We missed you this evening! It was great. The next one is on Tuesday — will you be there?”
