The Programme
Fast Start

Fast Start is your roadmap for the first 120 days. Each goal reached unlocks concrete rewards and lays the foundations of your business for years to come.

🎬 Getting-started video with Hilde — watch this first
Why the first 120 days are decisive

“Fast Start is a 4-step path designed to help you lay solid foundations for your Zinzino business.”

— Official Zinzino programme

The first 120 days set the trajectory of your business. They are not the hardest — they are the most important. They shape your habits, create your first results and prove to yourself that the model works. Act now, learn by doing.

Track your progress in your Back Office → tab Reporting → Fast Start. You see your progress on each of the 4 steps in real time.
The 4 Fast Start steps
Q-Team
30d
⚡ Step 1 — Days 0 to 30
Join the Q-Team
Goal: 4 customers subscribed to a First Kit (4 PCP and 20 credits).

This is your top priority from day 1. These 4 customers trigger your free Z4F, validate your model and give you momentum for what comes next.
🎁 One-off 30-day bonus: €100 + 10% Cash Bonus on each kit + free subscription (Z4F)
Enroll 2
60d
🤝 Step 2 — Days 31 to 60
Enrol 2 partners with a kit
Goal: 2 new partners enrolled with a kit — one on the left, one on the right of your organisation.

Help them start their own Fast Start immediately. Their start is your success.
🎁 +€400 network bonus (running total: €500)
Help 2
90d
🌱 Step 3 — Days 61 to 90
Help your 2 enrol their 2
Goal: Help each of your 2 partners enrol 2 new partners — that is 4 more people.

This is where duplication begins. You learn to train by supporting.
🎁 +€1,200 network bonus (running total: €1,700)
X-Team
120d
💎 Step 4 — Days 91 to 120
Join the X-Team
Goal: 10 personal customers (10 PCP / 50 credits).

The X-Team opens the door to monthly zRewards, the CAB Bonus and the ECB — the bonus that doubles your credits on new orders.
🎁 +€150 network bonus + 50 zRewards + free Z4F for life + ECB activated (estimated total: ~€2,500)

💎 For the ambitious — Diamond in 90 days

“Do every day what most people are not willing to do, and tomorrow you will live as most people cannot.”

— 90-Day Diamond Plan
⚡ Phase 1 — Days 1 to 30
Massive Launch
15 to 20 invitations a day (5C method)
2 to 3 presentations a day (Zoom, video, face to face)
2 team presentations a week
Daily training, 15 to 30 min
1 strategy meeting with your sponsor a week
Follow up with prospects within 24h
End-of-phase goal: 3 to 5 customers, 3 partners started, 1,000 points
🌱 Phase 2 — Days 31 to 60
Rapid Duplication
Work every day with your partners (coaching, follow-ups)
2 Zooms a week: 1 presentation + 1 training
2 direct calls with each new partner
Follow their Fast Start personally
Encourage the first product testimonials
End-of-phase goal: 3 active lines, 20 customers in total, 5,000 team points
💎 Phase 3 — Days 61 to 90
Consolidation
Daily coaching of your 3 leaders (15 min each)
Present, or have someone present, every day
Analyse the Back Office (volumes, subscriptions)
Encourage reading, gratitude and visualisation
Enrol 1 more new partner a week
End-of-phase goal: 3 leaders at 3,000 points = 10,000 points in total = Diamond ✅
