Why your story is your best tool

“100% of my customers’ first tests were out of balance. The blood test isn’t a promise — it’s proof. No placebo effect: concrete data about your biology.”

— Frédéric Pottecher, biologist, Marseille 2026

Arguments, figures, scientific studies — all of that matters. But what really builds trust with a prospect is your authentic personal story. Not a speech. Not a brochure. What you have lived, felt, measured.

The golden rule: Be the product of the product. Use the products every day, do your balance test, write down how you feel. Those notes will become your testimony.

💡 Rules for an authentic testimony
Never make medical promises — always talk about your personal experience.
Say “I noticed” rather than “it cures”.
An honest, humble testimony is always more powerful than a sales pitch.
Your results are yours — don’t inflate them, don’t play them down.
