Registration &
VDI Status (France)

Starting your business legally is simple and quick. In France, the VDI status (Independent Home Seller) was created specifically for you — with very few administrative constraints. Rules differ in each country: check with your sponsor.

📥 Download the document
What is the VDI status?

The Independent Home Seller (VDI) is a legal status created in France in 1993 specifically for direct selling. It lets you run your business as a Zinzino partner fully legally, with minimal paperwork and social contributions that increase progressively with your income.

The good news: You don’t need to set up a company. The VDI status is automatically managed by FISKA (Zinzino’s service provider), which handles the declarations and payment of your social contributions. Your only obligation: declare the start of your activity to INPI within 15 days.

Declaring your activity on the INPI website
⚠️
Legal deadlineYou have 15 days from the start of your activity to make this declaration. It is a legal obligation. The procedure takes about 20 minutes online.
1
Log in to the INPI website
Go to inpi.fr → Business formalities. Preferably choose France Connect+ (via La Poste digital identity or France Identité) — this will simplify future changes and closures, as this method already includes a qualified signature.
2
Select “Create a business” → Sole trader
Choose Sole trader. This name is general — it decides which administration your filing is sent to. The questions then become more specific.
3
Fill in the business activity details
Detailed description: Vente à domicile de compléments nutritionnels pour la marque Zinzino sous statut VDI (type it in French, exactly as shown)

Category 1: Commerce et artisanat de détail (retail trade and crafts)
Category 2: Vente à domicile (home selling)
Category 3: Mandataire (agent)
Category 4: Non-inscrit au RCS (not registered with the RCS)
4
Tax options — what to select
Profit taxation scheme: Régime spécial BNC (special BNC scheme)
VAT scheme: Franchise en base de TVA (VAT exemption)
Accrual accounting option: No
VAT liability on taxable operations: No
5
Submit and keep the acknowledgement of receipt
After submitting, you will receive a SIRET number and an acknowledgement of receipt. Keep these documents safe — they prove your legal status. FISKA will then take over automatically through Zinzino.
Special case — you already have a micro-enterprise
🔄 Combining VDI + micro-entrepreneur
It is entirely possible! If you already have a micro-enterprise for a separate activity, you can add the VDI activity as a secondary activity.

In that case, go to the INPI e-procedures portal → Modification → enter your SIREN number → Etablissements → Ajouter une activité (add an activity).

Answer NO to the questions “Activité principale” and “Activité principale de l’entreprise” (main activity). INPI agents can sometimes be thrown by this case — feel free to call them to confirm the right procedure (the FVD confirmed this approach with the INPI legal department).
⚠️
Important if you already have a micro-enterpriseINPI systems sometimes struggle with this multi-activity setup. We recommend contacting Fiska, who can do the filing for you for €20.
