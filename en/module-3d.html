The right mindset for objections

“An objection isn’t a refusal. It’s your prospect telling you: guide me better than I can guide myself.”

— Fundamental principle of network marketing

Objections are often fears in disguise : fear of making a mistake, fear of what others think, fear of failing again, fear of not being good enough. Your role isn’t to counter — it’s to reassure. That makes all the difference.

🛡️
The smokescreen objection
The first reason often isn’t the real one. “I don’t have time” can hide “I don’t trust this”. Dig deeper with: “What is really holding you back?”
💡
The information objection
The prospect lacks information. They need to be better informed, not forced into agreement. Give them the right information at the right time.
🚧
The condition objection
A genuine practical or financial obstacle. It deserves a creative, empathetic answer — not a sales pitch.
🎯
The interviewer’s posture
You are selecting, not begging. “Whether you’re interested or not doesn’t bother me.” This inner posture changes everything.
The 4-step method
1
Listen without interrupting
Let your prospect finish completely. Nod. Don’t look for the answer while they are talking. Active listening is already an answer in itself.
2
Rephrase and validate
“If I understand you correctly, what concerns you is…” — Rephrasing shows you have listened. It builds a bridge of trust before any answer.
3
Sincere empathy — share your own experience
“I understand how you feel, because I had that worry too…” — You were in their place. That sincerity is the only bridge that really works.
4
💎 Answer + validate
“…Then I looked into it, as you are doing now, and I realised that…” — Always finish by validating: “Does everything seem clearer now?”

⭐ The 7 classic objections — Reference answers
⏱“I don’t have time”Time▾
Reference answer
“I understand what you’re saying, because when this project was presented to me I was already very busy too! Then I looked into exactly how it works and realised that the leverage offered by relationship marketing actually solved my problem. With very little of my time, just what I need to invite a few people, plus the work of my sponsor and my support line, I’ve managed to build an extra income that some months already exceeds my main income!”
🔄 Oslo 2025 reframe

❌ “It doesn’t take long” → ✅ “I understand, it’s precisely because your time is limited that you need something flexible that delivers results. Let me show you how.”

⚡ The key point

Leverage is at the heart of the answer. You multiply your time thanks to the team — that’s the very principle of the model.

👥“I don’t know anyone”Network▾
Reference answer
“I understand, because when I discovered this project I had that worry too. Then I realised that two people are enough to start my team, and they don’t need to be close friends. What matters is teamwork and my own partners’ contacts. When I understood that, I made my decision and today I’m delighted! I’m offering you exactly that: a way to multiply your time!”
🔄 Oslo 2025 reframe

❌ “You need to know lots of people” → ✅ “Most people start without a network. That’s why we teach you step by step how to make new connections.”

⚡ The key point

Two people are enough. The power also comes from your partners’ networks, not only yours. That’s the network effect.

🔺“It’s a pyramid scheme”Trust▾
Reference answer
“I understand how you feel, because that’s what I thought at first too. Then I looked into it, as you are doing now. I understood that every human organisation has a pyramid shape: a family, a company, the state. An illegal pyramid scheme is a scam where the money comes only from high sign-up fees, with no real product. At Zinzino, 100% of income comes from the consumption of real, scientifically tested products. Relationship marketing has been legally regulated in France since 1993 (VDI status).”
🔄 Oslo 2025 reframe

❌ “No, it’s not a pyramid” → ✅ “We work with science-based products that give measurable results, within a regulated framework that rewards recommendation — just as Netflix pays influencers to share its brand.”

⚡ The key point

Don’t defend yourself. Educate. The legal distinction is simple and clear: pyramid scheme = a scam with no product. Relationship marketing = legal distribution with real products.

🎓“I don’t like selling”Skills▾
Reference answer
“I completely understand, because I don’t like selling either! I had the same fears when this project was presented to me. Then I was told that salespeople generally don’t do well in this business, because people don’t like feeling they’re being sold to. Our goal is to recommend, to make known the products we love and use ourselves. I simply do what I’ve always done: recommend what I like. People love buying a product as soon as they’re convinced it can be useful to them.”
🔄 Oslo 2025 reframe

❌ “It’s easy, you just recommend” → ✅ “It’s not about selling, but about sharing solutions and letting people measure their own results with the test.”

⚡ The key point

The BalanceTest is your best salesperson. The results speak for themselves — you don’t have to convince, just show.

🎓“I don’t have the skills”Skills▾
Reference answer
“I completely understand, because I didn’t feel qualified at the start either. Then I realised that skills are learned — nobody is born with them. What matters is the will to learn and the team’s support. You’ll have a sponsor, full training and ready-to-use tools. The learning curve is normal and you’re supported at every step.”
🔄 Oslo 2025 reframe

❌ “You don’t need to be an expert” → ✅ “You can rely on ready-made tools, tests and scientific studies, step by step. You don’t need to know everything — you need to know where to look.”

⚡ The key point

The system is duplicable. Your role isn’t to be a nutrition expert — it’s to be an expert in human connection.

💶“It’s too expensive”Money▾
Reference answer
“I understand it’s an investment. But let me ask you a question: how much is your less-than-optimal health costing you right now — in lost energy, medication, days when you don’t feel your best? The price is what you pay. The cost is what you lose by not acting. The BalanceTest will give you an objective, measured answer about your real situation before you spend anything.”
🔄 Price vs cost

The price is one-off and known. The cost of non-optimised health is invisible but real and ongoing. Make this distinction in your answer.

⚡ The key point

Never defend the price. Defend the value. The blood test is objective proof that the product is needed — before you even start.

💰“Can you really make money?”Money▾
Reference answer
“That’s the right question to ask. Income depends directly on the time you invest and your consistency. It isn’t getting rich quick — it’s building step by step. The first months are for learning the system and laying the foundations. After that, income becomes recurring and grows. The compensation plan is transparent, verifiable and the company is listed on the stock market — everything is public.”
⚡ The key point

Be honest about timescales. Promising quick money destroys your credibility. Promising a solid, gradual system strengthens it.

The prospect sees the data themselves — you don’t have to convince them. That objective proof changes everything in the approach and in customer retention rates.”

⏱ Time & Organisation
⏳“I’ll think about it”Time▾
Answer
“Of course, it’s normal to take your time. What exactly do you feel you need to think about? It would help me give you the right information.”
⚡ Why this question

“I’ll think about it” is almost always a hidden objection. By asking “about what?”, you find the real resistance and can truly answer it. Then suggest a precise date to call back.

📅“It’s not the right time”Time▾
Answer
“I completely understand. When do you think would be the right time for you?”

[After the answer]: “Perfect. I’ll get back in touch then. May I put [date] in my diary to call you back?”
⚡ Always finish with a date

Without a precise call-back date, the conversation is over. With a date, it’s on pause. The difference is huge in the long run.

🧪 Product & Health
🥗“I already eat healthily, I don’t need it”Story▾
Answer
“That’s exactly what I thought too! And that’s why I was surprised by my test result — I was careful about what I ate. The test doesn’t measure what you eat, it measures what your cells have actually absorbed and used. 97% of people tested are out of balance, even those who eat healthily. The test will give you an objective answer about your real situation.”
⚡ The key figure

97% of people tested have an Omega-6/Omega-3 imbalance. Even people who are careful. This figure, from 1.4 million tests, is your most powerful argument.

👨‍⚕️“My doctor doesn’t know about this”Story▾
Answer
“That’s completely normal — doctors are trained to treat diseases, rarely in cellular nutritional prevention. Yet research on Omega-3 and O6/O3 ratios is well documented scientifically — more than 30,000 published studies. Zinzino’s study of 500,000 tests was published on ResearchGate. If your doctor is open-minded, share this data with them — some are very interested.”
⚡ Never criticise the doctor

Position yourself as complementary to medicine, never in opposition. The test-based approach is preventive, not curative.
