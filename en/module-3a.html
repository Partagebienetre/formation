The right mindset

“You are not in the product business. You are in the prospecting business.”

— Michael J. Durkin

Before writing down the first name, understand why this list is your most precious tool. It isn’t a list of “victims” to convince — it’s a list of people to whom you will offer something of value, leaving them free to say yes or no.

🧹
Clear your mind
As long as a name stays in your head, it takes up mental space. Once written down, it is safe and frees up room.
🔗
The trigger effect
One name leads to another. Write down Mary → you think of her husband → then of her colleague. The list grows by itself.
💎
Your business capital
This asset doesn’t lose value. Unlike a product stock, it grows richer, gets qualified and is worked on over time.
🚫
Never judge on someone else’s behalf
Your role is to write down, not to decide. The people you thought least interested sometimes become your best partners.
The golden rule: A list of 200 names is better than 10 “perfect” names. Quality comes from quantity. Write everything down, sort afterwards. Minimum goal: 100 contacts / names / prospects to start.

The pipeline — your contacts’ statuses

Every contact in your CRM has a status. This pipeline lets you know exactly where each person is and what the next action is.

1🟡 Thought of
Written down, not contacted yet
2🟢 Contact details
Found, ready to invite
3🔵 Invited

4🟣 Presented
Attended a presentation
5🩷 Thinking it over
Interested, decision pending
6💎 Partner/Customer
Has joined or ordered
What to do — and what to avoid
✅ Do
Write everyone down, without exception
Always think of the spouse / partner
Update the CRM after every contact
Present to the couple when there is one
Plan follow-ups in advance
Always keep an active list
🚫 Avoid
Deciding on someone else’s behalf
Prospecting in fits and starts
Presenting the concept over the phone
Leaving contacts without a planned follow-up
Stopping at 20 or 30 names
Excluding someone “because it won’t work for them”
🎯 The daily list routine
Morning — Identify 3 contacts / names / prospects to work on today.
Daytime — Make your calls or send your messages (30 minutes minimum).
Evening — Update the CRM, plan tomorrow’s follow-ups.

The 9 deep reasons for joining network marketing

When you talk to a contact / name / prospect, look for their deep reason. These 9 motivations cover the vast majority of profiles. Identifying the right one changes everything in your approach.

💰
1. Earning extra income
The most common motivation. A concrete, immediate extra income you can measure every month.
🏆
2. Enjoying financial freedom
No longer depending on a single employer. Having recurring income that works even when you don’t.
🏢
3. Owning your own business
The wish to be an entrepreneur without the risks and constraints of a traditional start-up. Being your own boss.
⏰
4. Having more free time
Choosing your hours, being there for your family, no longer being a slave to an imposed schedule.
🌱
5. Speeding up your personal growth
Learning communication, leadership, team management. A real school of life, free of charge.
🤝
6. Helping others
Sharing products that change people’s health. Supporting partners towards their success.
👥
7. Making new contacts
Building a rich human network, meeting inspiring people, breaking out of social isolation.
🛡️
8. Securing a better retirement
Building, starting now, a passive income that keeps growing even after your working life.
🌟
9. Leaving a legacy
Creating something that lasts, passing on to your children financial freedom and a different way of thinking.
How to use these 9 motivations: In your conversations, ask open questions and listen. When you identify your contact’s deep motivation, adapt your invitation to what they are really looking for — not to what you want to sell them.
Asking for referrals

“A list should never run dry. Every person you meet — whether they join or not — can open the door to 5 other people.”

— Fundamental principle of network marketing

Whether the answer is yes or no, always ask for referrals. It is the most powerful technique to grow your list endlessly without ever running out of contacts.

📋 When to ask for referrals
After a yes — “Now that you’re in, who around you might also be interested?”

After a no — “I completely understand. Even if it isn’t for you right now, do you know anyone around you looking to earn extra income?”

After a presentation — “Whether you go for it or not, who is the first person who comes to mind when you think about what you’ve just seen?”
🔄
Short referral script
“Who do you know who is looking to improve their health or earn extra income? Just a first name is enough.”
🎯
Targeted referral script
“Among your contacts, do you know someone dynamic, who likes people and would be open to a new idea?”
💡
Script after a refusal
“No problem at all! By the way, is there anyone in your family or among your friends who might be interested in this kind of concept?”
📱
Message script
“It may not be the right time for you, that’s completely normal. But who around you might be interested?”
